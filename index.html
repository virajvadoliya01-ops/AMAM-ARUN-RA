Every recommendation passes through a documented five-step audit. Nothing is shortcut. No leveraged punts, no quick tips.

01

Quantitative Screening

Quantitative filters across ~4,000 listed Indian companies assessing balance sheet health, return on equity (ROE > 18%), cash conversion cycles, and debt-to-equity ratios.

02

Deep Forensic Analysis

Auditing 10-year annual reports, investor conference calls, promoter background checks, channel checks, and forensic accounting to eliminate corporate governance red flags.

03

Valuation Framework

Discounted Cash Flow (DCF), peer multiples, and reverse-engineered expectations to calculate margin-of-safety price ranges rather than single speculative targets.

04

Every research note carries an explicit, documented list of operational, regulatory, and macro risks that would invalidate the thesis and trigger a disciplined exit.

Ongoing Coverage & Quarterly Review

Post-earnings reviews on all covered companies every quarter—recommendations are upgraded, held, or exited with published rationale.
